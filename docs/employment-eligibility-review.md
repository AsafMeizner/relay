## Finding
The official rules contain no blanket exclusion for government employees or military personnel. Section 3 excludes promotion entities and their employees, representatives and agents; people involved in running the event; judges and employers of judges; relevant affiliates; and anyone whose participation creates a real or apparent conflict in the organizer's discretion. Israel is not expressly geographically excluded.

Consequently, IDF employment by itself is not an express disqualifier under the text reviewed. Eligibility remains conditional: we have not verified all judge affiliations, the owner's duties, employer permission or intellectual-property obligations. Do not describe the owner as unconditionally eligible.

## Ownership and project boundaries
Section 4 requires original work solely owned by the entrant, with no other person's or entity's rights or interest, and no breach of contract, privacy or intellectual-property rights. Technical assistance is permitted subject to those conditions. The project uses synthetic incidents; no employer data, branding, systems or confidential material is needed.

The final owner approval should confirm personal participation, authority to publish the work and absence of relevant sponsor/judge/employer conflicts. If the owner identifies an actual ambiguity, Section 11 requires a written clarification request before the deadline; the owner would send it. No new request is necessary merely because their occupation is military.
